# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Anyone who needs a short link.** Members of the public who arrive, paste a URL, copy the result and leave. No account needed for a basic link.
- **Developers.** People who create links through the REST API with API keys, read the Swagger docs, or browse the source on GitHub.
- **People evaluating the author's work.** hrva.cc is a HrvaLabs piece; the engineering blog posts, the changelog and the public status page show how it is built and run.
- **The author and people they know.** Day-to-day personal use; the site is open to everyone else.

## Product Purpose

hrva.cc shortens URLs and lets registered users manage them: custom aliases, visit limits, expiry dates, password protection, tags, QR codes, per-link analytics, API keys and two-factor login. Destination URLs are checked against Google Safe Browsing. Admins get user, URL, API key, health, audit log and email log views.

Success: a visitor gets a working short link in seconds, and someone looking at the site can tell a real person built it with care.

## Positioning

A free, ad-free, non-commercial shortener built and run by one person in Croatia (HrvaLabs), with its full source on GitHub. It is privacy-first in a way that can be checked in that source: visitor IPs are bcrypt-hashed on arrival and the hashes are deleted after 48 hours.

## Operating Context

- Live at hrva.cc (app at app.hrva.cc); Next.js 16 on Cloudflare Workers; Spring Boot backend in `../url-shortener`.
- Public API docs at `https://hrva.cc/swagger-ui/index.html`; status page at `https://status.hrva.cc/status/url`; source at `https://github.com/karlohrvacic/url-shortener`.
- Running since February 2022; changelog served from the backend at `/api/changelog`.
- Sign-in by email/password or Google OAuth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Anonymous users can shorten a URL; aliases, limits, expiry, analytics and management need an account.
- `/validate/[short]` lets anyone peek at a short link's destination before visiting.
- Light and dark themes with a toggle must remain.
- Easter eggs (`/fortune`, `/egg`, Konami code) are part of the product.
- Fonts are self-hosted via `next/font`; nothing should load third-party assets on page load.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **hrva.cc**, a **HrvaLabs** project (link to https://hrvalabs.net/).
- Light/dark toggle stays. Everything else about the look, including the logo mark and amber colour, may be replaced.
- Visual register (chosen October 2026): the familiar modern URL-shortener standard, held to the craft level of Dub (dub.co) and Short.io. The owner asked for "modern, refreshing" and for it not to look AI-generated; avoid the dark-amber-glow, glass, gradient-text and serif-display look it replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real features listed above; real blog posts in `content/blog/posts.ts`; real changelog from the API.
- Privacy facts from the backend: IP bcrypt hashing (`DefaultIPAddressService`), 2-hour unique-visitor window, 48-hour deletion of hashed records.
- No user counts, link counts, testimonials, uptime figures or speed benchmarks are on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The shorten box is the product; nothing stands between a visitor and a link.
2. Say only what can be verified in the source.
3. It should feel made by a person, not generated by a template.
4. Developers are first-class: the API, docs and source are one click away.
